# T Mobile Special Business Review

As of September 2023, T-Mobile currently imposes a **200K per day message cap on every 10DLC Brand** sending Messaging traffic to its customers.

To exceed this threshold you must successfully pass a Special Business Review.

**MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS:**

1. **A Vetted (*not* only Verified) 10DLC Brand.** Your Brand must have been Vetted by a third party that the TCR endorses. You can do this via the Portal or Telnyx API.
2. 10DLC **Brand Traffic** that is **likely to exceed 200K messages per day on a regular basis**.
3. A Primary Use Case
4. A Secondary Use Case

**FEES & TIMELINES:**

1. The expected **turnaround time is 2-3 months** (as of September 2023)
2. A willingness to pay necessary fees.

   1. As of September 2023, there were no fees, however...
   2. **In the past there was a $5000 Review Fee** charged by T-Mobile [which is on hold until further notice]

If your Brand meets the Requirements and you are comfortable with the Timelines (plus any possible fees), please [Contact Support](http://telnyx.com/contact-us) to request a Special Business Review.
